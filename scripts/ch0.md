## S0.1 · A stick on the plane
`id: s0_1` · `target: 1:00` · `time: 0:00–1:06 (est.)`

**NARRATION**
> Here is a rule. [beat] Take a stick exactly one unit long, and drop it anywhere on a flat plane that goes on forever. Wherever it lands, its two ends must sit on different colours. [beat] Your job is to paint the entire plane, every single point, so that the rule never breaks, no matter where the stick falls. [beat] How many colours do you need? [beat2] This question is more than seventy-five years old. For most of that time, the honest answer was: somewhere between four and seven. In 2018, it became five to seven. [beat] And in September 2026, a sixty-two-page preprint, credited to OpenAI and produced by one of its internal models, claimed that five is not enough either. [beat] This video is about how anyone could prove something like that, what exactly was proved, and what we do and don't know about how it was found. [beat] Let's start by playing the game.
