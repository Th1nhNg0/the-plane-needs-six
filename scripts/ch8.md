## S8.2 · What this does and doesn't show
`id: s8_2` · `target: 1:30` · `time: 38:22–39:29 (est.)`

**NARRATION**
> What does this release show about AI and mathematics? [beat] It shows that an internal OpenAI model can produce a long, technical proof that engages with decades of published work, and whose main claim has been formalized and, by OpenAI's report, checked by machine. [beat] It took a different road from the one the field had been on since 2018: around the search for finite graphs, rather than further down it. [beat] What it doesn't show is how often the model gets things wrong. Only selected results were released, and OpenAI itself warns that some unformalized results may have issues. It doesn't show how much human guidance was involved, or how the effort compares with human research. Those details aren't public. [beat2] What we can see is this: a question from 1950, a little figure from 1961, and a proof from 2026 that uses that figure to finish its argument. [beat] Six, or seven. That one is still open.
